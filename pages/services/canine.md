## What are the benefits of physiotherapy for your dog?

Physiotherapy can be a valuable part of your dog’s overall health and wellbeing, helping to improve strength, mobility, movement and physical function. It can also play an important role in injury prevention, helping your dog stay active and maintain good physical condition throughout their life.

For dogs recovering from an injury or surgery, physiotherapy provides structured, professional support throughout every stage of rehabilitation. Imogen will create an individualised, evidence-based treatment and exercise plan based on your dog’s specific needs, helping them recover safely, build confidence and return to the activities they love.

Physiotherapy can also be particularly beneficial for older dogs, helping to maintain mobility, manage stiffness and support comfort, allowing them to stay active and enjoy a good quality of life for longer.

## What conditions can physiotherapy help with?

Physiotherapy can be beneficial for a wide range of musculoskeletal and neurological conditions, including:

- Hip dysplasia
- Elbow dysplasia
- Osteoarthritis
- IVDD (intervertebral disc disease)
- Wobbler syndrome
- Patella luxation
- Trigger points
- Contracture deformities
- Muscle injuries
- Bone fractures
- Cruciate disease
- Tendon injuries

Every dog is unique, which is why all treatments are individually tailored. Following a thorough assessment, a personalised treatment and exercise programme will be created to address your dog’s specific needs and rehabilitation goals.

## What can you expect during a physiotherapy session?

Each physiotherapy session begins with a thorough assessment of your dog. This includes observing their gait and posture, as well as carrying out a detailed palpation and range-of-motion assessment.

The findings from this assessment are then used to develop a clinically reasoned and individually tailored treatment plan. This ensures that your dog receives the most appropriate care for their current condition, needs and stage of rehabilitation.

- Joint and soft tissue mobilisation
- Trigger point release
- Heat therapy
- Electrotherapies, including LASER and TENS
- Therapeutic exercise
- Balance and proprioceptive exercises

## Exercise prescription

Exercise prescription is a key part of successful rehabilitation. At the end of each physiotherapy session, you will be provided with a selection of exercises specifically tailored to your dog. These may be designed to improve strength, balance, flexibility, coordination and overall mobility.

Each exercise will be demonstrated to you during the session, giving you the opportunity to ask questions and make sure you feel confident carrying them out correctly at home.

Your dog’s exercise programme will be reviewed and adapted as they progress, ensuring their rehabilitation continues to be appropriate and effective at every stage.
