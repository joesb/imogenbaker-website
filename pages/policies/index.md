To ensure everything runs smoothly and that both you and your horse or animal are well cared for, the following Terms and Conditions apply to all Veterinary Physiotherapy and Equine Coaching services provided by Imogen Baker.

## Veterinary Referral and Consent

In accordance with the Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966 and the Veterinary Surgery (Exemptions) Order 2015, physiotherapy treatment for animals must be carried out under the direction of a qualified veterinary surgeon who has examined the animal and prescribed physiotherapy treatment.

A completed and signed veterinary referral or consent form must therefore be provided prior to the initial physiotherapy assessment for any rehabilitation cases. This ensures that treatment is carried out appropriately and as part of a vet-led approach to your animal’s care.

## Equine Coaching

Equine coaching is tailored to the individual horse and rider, taking into consideration their experience, ability and goals.

The owner/rider is responsible for providing a suitable and safe environment for the lesson and ensuring that the horse is fit and suitable for the planned activities.

Any concerns regarding your horse’s health, soundness or ability to participate in a lesson should be discussed with Imogen before the session. If a horse is considered unsuitable to work, the session may be adapted or postponed where appropriate.

Riders are responsible for wearing suitable riding attire and appropriate safety equipment, including a correctly fitted and correct standard riding hat.

## Payment Terms

Payment must be made in full:

If any treatments are being funded by insurance please contact Imogen prior to the appointment to confirm any details.

Appointments and lessons are not considered fully paid until payment has been received.

## Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. However, appointments and lessons are reserved specifically for you, so sufficient notice is appreciated for cancellations.

**Veterinary Physiotherapy appointments:**{.h3-style}

- More than 24 hours' notice: No cancellation charge.
- Less than 24 hours' notice: A £35 cancellation fee will be charged.

**Equine Coaching lessons:**{.h3-style}

- More than 24 hours' notice: No cancellation charge.
- Less than 24 hours' notice: A £20 cancellation fee will be charged.

Where possible, I will always do my best to rearrange your appointment or lesson to another suitable date.

## Weather and Safety

For equine coaching, lessons may need to be postponed or adapted due to unsuitable weather or unsafe conditions. The safety of both horse and rider will always be the priority.

Imogen reserves the right to stop or modify a session if conditions are considered unsafe or if continuing would be inappropriate for the horse or rider.

## Appointments, Treatment and Coaching

All physiotherapy treatment and exercise programmes are tailored to the individual animal following an appropriate assessment.

Coaching sessions are also tailored to the individual horse and rider, with exercises selected according to their ability, goals and the horse’s physical needs.

Any advice or exercises provided should be followed as directed. If your animal’s condition changes, deteriorates or you have any concerns following treatment or exercise, you should contact your veterinary surgeon for further advice.

## Owner and Rider Responsibility

The owner/rider is responsible for providing accurate and relevant information regarding their horse or animal, including any known medical conditions, injuries, behavioural concerns or previous treatment.

It is the responsibility of the owner/rider to inform Imogen of any changes to their animal’s health or circumstances that may affect treatment or coaching.
