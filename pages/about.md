She gained her British Horse Society (BHS) stage exams throughout her youth and early adult years. Her passion for rehabilitation began while working for a charity rescue and outreach organisation and continued to grow during her Undergraduate Degree in Bioveterinary Science at the Royal Veterinary College (RVC). Imogen has now completed her Postgraduate Diploma and is now qualified in Veterinary Physiotherapy. She plans to continue her research this year in order to complete her Master’s Degree.{.content-canvas-item-left}

Imogen is a proud member of the Institute of Registered Veterinary & Animal Physiotherapist’s (IRVAP) as well as a BHS Accredited Professional Coach (BHS APC).

## Qualifications

- PGDip in Veterinary Physiotherapy
- BSc (Hons) in Bioveterinary Science
- BHS Stage 4 Yard Manager
- BHS Stage 3 Coach in Complete Horsemanship
- BHS Stage 2 Foundation Coach in Complete Horsemanship
- BHS Stage 1 in Complete Horsemanship
